# Gargoyle Dungeons

A dungeon journal for WoW Forever, in the Gargoyle window. Needs the **Gargoyle** addon.

## What it does

- **Every dungeon and raid**: levels, size and where it is, including the new ones Forever adds, and when
  the ones still to come open.
- **Bosses**: each boss in order, with its loot (and drop chances) and its abilities in the game's own words.
- **Quests**: each dungeon's quests for your side and class, who gives them and where, and the whole chain of
  quests before them, with the steps you've done ticked off. "Show on map" puts the game's own map pin on the
  next one to do, only when you click it.
- **Map**: the game's own maps of Kalimdor and the Eastern Kingdoms with a pin on every entrance. Point at a pin
  to see the place, click it to open it.
- **Your upgrades**: drops and quest rewards that are upgrades for your character show how much better they
  are. Ctrl-click a drop to try it on, Shift-click to link it.

It opens on the dungeon you're in. Open it from the Gargoyle window's **Dungeons** tab.

Everything in the journal works on its own. The upgrade marks come from your character on
[gargoyle.gg](https://gargoyle.gg), brought in by the free Gargoyle app ([gargoyle.gg/addon](https://gargoyle.gg/addon)).
The same journal is on the website: [gargoyle.gg/dungeons](https://gargoyle.gg/dungeons).

## Safe to use

Only the game's own addon features: it shows things, and the map pin and dressing room are the game's own,
only when you click. No ads, nothing automated, never needs a paid account.

Free and open source (GPL-3.0): [source code on GitHub](https://github.com/Gargoyle-GG/Gargoyle).
