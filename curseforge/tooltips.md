# Gargoyle Damage Tooltips

Adds a breakdown of your spells' damage and healing to their tooltips, worked out from your character's own
stats, like the gear planner on [gargoyle.gg](https://gargoyle.gg):

- the spell's base numbers
- your spell power's share
- what your talents add
- your crit
- the average per cast, per second and per mana

Works on its own. With the **Gargoyle** addon, you can switch it on or off in Options > AddOns > Gargoyle.

## Safe to use

It only adds lines to the game's own tooltips. No ads, nothing automated, never needs a paid account.

Free and open source (GPL-3.0): [source code on GitHub](https://github.com/Gargoyle-GG/Gargoyle).
