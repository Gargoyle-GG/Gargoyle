# Gargoyle Loot

Every item in WoW Forever right now and where it comes from, in the Gargoyle window. Needs the
**Gargoyle** addon.

## What it does

- **Browse by where items come from**: all dungeons, all raids, merchants and reputation (with the standing
  each item needs), professions (with skill and reagents) and item sets (with their bonuses), or just one
  dungeon, profession, faction or set.
- **Search** within any of them, and filter by slot, armor or weapon type, what your character can use and
  your level.
- **Your upgrades**: items that are upgrades for your character show how much better they are, and
  "Upgrades only" lists just those.
- **Try it on**: Ctrl-click an item to see it on your character in the game's dressing room, or try on a whole
  set at once. Shift-click links it in chat. Pointing at an item shows where it comes from and what you wear
  in that slot now (hold Shift to compare).
- **Wishlist**: star the items you want. Your wishlist is the same one as on
  [gargoyle.gg/loot](https://gargoyle.gg/loot), and Gargoyle can tell you which wishlist items drop in the
  dungeon you go into.

Only gear that's in the game now is listed: nothing above the current level cap, and nothing from raids that
aren't released yet.

Browsing, searching and trying items on work on their own. Upgrade marks and the wishlist come from your
Gargoyle account, through the free Gargoyle app ([gargoyle.gg/addon](https://gargoyle.gg/addon)).

## Safe to use

Only the game's own addon features: it shows things, and the dressing room and chat link are the game's own,
only when you click. No ads, nothing automated, never needs a paid account.

Free and open source (GPL-3.0): [source code on GitHub](https://github.com/Gargoyle-GG/Gargoyle).
