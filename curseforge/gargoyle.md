# Gargoyle

The in-game side of **[gargoyle.gg](https://gargoyle.gg)**, the free WoW Forever companion: talent and gear
planner, dungeon journal, loot lists and guild tools.

## What it does

- **Raid signups.** Your guild's upcoming raids, who's coming by role, and Coming / Tentative / Can't come
  buttons, without leaving the game. Officers can make new raids in game. Days with raids get a small mark on
  the game's own calendar, and you can get a reminder a day before a raid you haven't signed up for.
- **Your characters, kept up to date.** Pick the characters you want on your Gargoyle account: their level,
  gear, talents, enchants and professions follow as you play, ready for the gear planner.
- **Talent plans.** Follow a talent build from your account: the game's talent window marks the talents it
  still wants. You still pick every talent yourself.
- **Upgrades and your wishlist on item tooltips.** Item tooltips anywhere say when an item is an upgrade for
  your character (and by how much), and when it's on your wishlist.

Open it with the Gargoyle button on the minimap, or `/gargoyle`. Each feature can be switched off in
Options > AddOns > Gargoyle.

## More Gargoyle addons

- **Gargoyle Dungeons**: every dungeon and raid's bosses, loot, abilities and quests, and a map of where they are.
- **Gargoyle Loot**: every item and where it comes from, with search, your upgrades and a wishlist.
- **Gargoyle Damage Tooltips**: a breakdown of your spells' damage and healing on their tooltips.

## The Gargoyle app

Addons can't go online, so your raids, characters, upgrades and wishlist come and go through the free
**Gargoyle app** for Windows. It also installs and updates the Gargoyle addons for you. Get it at
[gargoyle.gg/addon](https://gargoyle.gg/addon).

## Safe to use

Gargoyle only uses the game's own addon features. It shows things and records your clicks: it never plays for
you, never sends chat messages, and never reads game files or the game's memory. No ads, nothing in it costs
money, and it never needs a paid account.

Free and open source (GPL-3.0): [source code on GitHub](https://github.com/Gargoyle-GG/Gargoyle).
